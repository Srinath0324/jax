JAX

A personal AI second brain.

JAX is a private assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It keeps track of what matters, takes care of repetitive work, and brings the right thing up at the right time.

What JAX does

  • Remembers notes, ideas and decisions, and finds them again when needed
  • Keeps a daily list of what needs attention
  • Automates routine computer tasks
  • Sends reminders and follow-ups
  • Always asks before taking any important action

Privacy policy

JAX is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

  • When JAX connects to a Google account, it asks for read-only access and uses it only to show information to the owner.
  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising.
  • JAX does not send, change or delete anything in a connected account.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
